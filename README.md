# LedgerAI

LedgerAI is an ongoing project focused on making personal finance easier to understand and manage.

The idea is to take everyday financial data, such as bank statements, and turn it into something more useful. LedgerAI aims to help users understand where their money is going by organizing and categorizing transactions automatically.

The project is also exploring how AI can be used to assist with financial insights and investment analysis. One of the goals is to analyze available stocks based on a user's investment budget and shortlist potential options for further consideration.

## Vision

The vision behind LedgerAI is to build a simple personal finance platform that helps users better understand their spending, manage their money, and explore investment opportunities without making the experience unnecessarily complicated.

## Current Progress

Currently, LedgerAI is being developed as an ongoing project.

Some of the features being worked on include:

- Uploading and processing bank statements
- Normalizing transaction descriptions
- Automatically categorizing transactions using AI
- Storing previously identified transaction patterns
- Analyzing stocks based on an available investment budget
- Using market data and AI to compare potential investment options
